ASSIGNMENT 1: LOGIN TO A GMAIL ACCOUNT

Algorithm:
1. Start.
2. Open a web browser.
3. Visit the Gmail website (https://mail.google.com).
4. Click "Sign in".
5. Enter the Gmail email address.
6. Click "Next".
7. Enter the password.
8. Click "Next".
9. Check whether the login details are accepted.
10. If the details are correct, open the Gmail inbox.
11. If the details are incorrect, display the error and ask the user to try again.
12. Stop.
